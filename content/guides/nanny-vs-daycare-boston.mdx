We did both in one year: a nanny, then daycare when our nanny had a family emergency. Here's what we wish we'd seen side by side.

## The cost, side by side

| | Daycare (infant, center) | Full-time nanny |
|---|---|---|
| **Typical yearly cost** | about $27,000 statewide (2025); around $31,000 in Boston | about $55,000–70,000+ in wages |
| **Extra costs** | Registration, supplies, late pickups | Employer taxes (~8–9%), workers' comp, agency fee if used |
| **Second child** | Roughly doubles the cost | Usually a modest raise |
| **Where the numbers come from** | Child Care Aware of America (2025 state prices); DaycareCalc Boston estimate | Boston wage sources ([see wages](/guides/boston-nanny-wages)) |

Massachusetts has the highest average price for infant center care of any state that reported data in 2025, according to Child Care Aware of America. You can download the Massachusetts fact sheet from their report.

## When daycare usually wins

- **One child**, especially an infant or toddler: it's usually the cheaper option.
- **Socializing and structure:** classrooms, routines, other kids.
- **Reliability:** no single point of failure if a caregiver is sick.
- **Simplicity:** you're a customer, not an employer. No payroll, no taxes, no work agreement.

## When a nanny usually wins

- **Two or more kids:** one nanny can cost less than two daycare spots.
- **Long or odd hours:** daycare hours are fixed; a nanny can match your schedule.
- **Sick days:** daycare sends sick kids home; a nanny can stay with them.
- **No waitlist:** infant daycare spots in Boston can take months to open up.
- **Nanny share:** two families split one nanny, which often brings the per-family cost close to daycare.

## Questions that usually decide it

1. **How many kids will need care in the next two years?**
2. **What hours do you actually need,** including commutes?
3. **What happens on a sick day?** Who stays home?
4. **How soon do you need care?** Get on daycare waitlists early, even if you're unsure.
5. **Do you want to be an employer?** A nanny means payroll and paperwork ([see the taxes guide](/guides/massachusetts-nanny-taxes)).

## Ways families bring the cost down

- **Nanny share** with a neighbor.
- **Part-time nanny + part-time daycare.**
- **Dependent care FSA:** up to $7,500 pre-tax in 2026, for either daycare or a nanny.
- **Child and dependent care tax credit** on part of your costs.

There's no wrong answer. Many families, including ours, end up trying more than one.
