Hiring a full-time nanny in Boston through an agency costs about $77,000–92,000 in the first year: $62,000–73,000 in wages, about $5,000–6,000 in employer taxes, and a one-time agency placement fee of $10,000–14,000, plus workers' comp insurance. Hiring on your own at job-site rates costs about $51,000. The placement fee is paid once, so year two through an agency costs about $68,000–79,000. (Full-time means 40 hours a week for one toddler; try your own numbers in the [estimator](/estimate).)

Here's how it breaks down, so you can plan for your own situation.

## The four parts of the cost

| Part | Typical Boston cost (2026) | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Wages** | $25–35+ an hour for a career nanny; about $55,000–70,000+ a year full-time | [What Boston nannies earn](/guides/boston-nanny-wages) |
| **2. Employer taxes** | About 8–9% on top of wages (about $4,400 on $52,000) | [Nanny taxes in Massachusetts](/guides/massachusetts-nanny-taxes) |
| **3. Workers' comp** | Required at 16+ hours a week; price varies by insurer | [Nanny taxes in Massachusetts](/guides/massachusetts-nanny-taxes) |
| **4. Agency fee** (optional) | 15–18% of first-year pay plus a $300–500 upfront fee, at the two Boston agencies that publish theirs | [What nanny agencies charge](/guides/nanny-agency-fees-boston) |

## A full-time example

A nanny at **$28 an hour, 45 hours a week** (40 regular + 5 overtime at 1.5×), all year:

| | Yearly |
|---|---|
| Wages | about $69,000 |
| Employer taxes (~8–9%) | about $5,500–6,200 |
| Workers' comp | varies |
| **Total, without an agency** | **about $75,000** |
| Agency fee at 15–18% plus upfront (if used) | about $10,700–13,000 more, one time |

## Why the numbers you've seen disagree

Job sites list **asking rates** (about $21–25 an hour in Boston), and many listings are students or part-timers. Experienced nannies placed by agencies typically earn **$30–35 an hour**. Some sites also add employer taxes into the hourly figure and others don't. Ours shows each piece separately.

## Ways to lower the cost

- **Nanny share:** split one nanny with another family.
- **Part-time nanny plus daycare.**
- **Dependent care FSA:** up to **$7,500** pre-tax in 2026.
- **Hire without an agency:** you save the fee but do the screening yourself.

## Nanny or daycare?

For one child, daycare usually costs less: infant center care in Massachusetts averaged about **$27,000 a year** in 2025, and Boston runs higher. With two kids, the math often flips. [Compare nanny and daycare →](/guides/nanny-vs-daycare-boston)
