# Discovery Scripts

## A. Agency interview (20 min) — target 5 Boston agencies before launch

**Opener:** "I'm building a site that helps Boston families compare nanny options and price, and then sends qualified families to a small set of trusted agencies. I'm not trying to replace agencies; I'm trying to send you better-qualified families. Can I ask how your business works today?"

| ID | Question | Hypothesis | Listen for (revealed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | "Where do your families come from today? Roughly what share comes from each source?" | H1 | Referrals vs. paid search vs. directories; any mention of ChatGPT |
| A2 | "If I sent you 10 more qualified families next month, could you place them?" | H1 | **Waitlist = red flag.** "Yes, we need nannies, not families" = supply-constrained |
| A3 | "What do you spend to get one new family today, in money or time?" | H1 | A dollar figure or a named channel spend |
| A4 | "Would you pay per qualified brief, or a share of the placement fee? What would make a brief 'qualified'?" | H1, H2 | A number; the fields they need |
| A5 | "What's your fee structure: placement fee, guarantee period, hourly ranges you see?" | H3 | Willingness to put it in writing |
| A6 | "If families could see several agencies' prices side by side, how would you feel?" | H7 | Defensive = H7 risk; competitive = good |
| A7 | "Would you respond to a standardized brief within 48 hours?" | H2 | Commitment + a named contact |
| A8 | "Do families come back to you for temporary or travel care? How often?" | H6 | Evidence that the need repeats |

**Close:** "Can I send you 2–3 real briefs in the next month and see how they convert?" (A yes, plus a contact email, counts as H7 behavior.)

**Log every interview** as a row in `data/agencies.csv`.

## B. Family interview (15 min) — target 5 Boston parents (from your network, and brief submitters after launch)

| ID | Question | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | "Walk me through the last time you looked for childcare. Where did you start?" | H4, H5 |
| F2 | "Did you use ChatGPT, Claude or another AI for any part of it?" | H4 |
| F3 | "How did you figure out what you should pay? How confident were you?" | H5 |
| F4 | "What did the agency do that you couldn't have done yourself? What did you resent?" | H5, H7 |
| F5 | "Since then, have you needed care again (travel, sick days, a new baby)? What did you do?" | H6 |
| F6 | "Would you keep a private profile somewhere so options came to you next time? What would make that feel safe?" | H6 |
