# Hypotheses & Kill Criteria

Each hypothesis is a load-bearing part of the value thesis. If it fails, the part of the thesis it supports fails with it.
Thresholds are **proposed starting points**. Jonathan should set the final numbers before launch and not move them afterwards.

| # | Value-thesis claim it tests | Hypothesis | How we test | Proposed pass | Kill / pivot if | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Routing fee is capturable | Boston agencies are **demand-constrained** and will pay for qualified family demand | Agency discovery (Q-A1–A4) | ≥3 of 5 say they want more families; ≥2 would pay per brief or placement | Most are waitlisted or say referrals are enough → routing has no buyer; pivot to the family-side tool | G0 |
| H2 | Interoperability / future bidding | Agencies will **quote against a standardized brief** within 48h | Forward real briefs; log response time and quote | ≥70% respond within 48h with a price or next step | <40% respond → the brief format or the relationship is broken | G0 → G2 |
| H3 | Data flywheel (Zillow gap) | Agencies will **share pricing and fees** (rates, placement fee, guarantee period) | Discovery Q-A5; quote logs | ≥3 agencies share their fee structure | Nobody shares → cost data comes only from families; weaker moat | G0 |
| H4 | Distribution moves to agents | AI assistants **route families** to an agent-legible site | Referrer tracking (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, perplexity.ai, etc.), "how did you find us," API/MCP submissions | ≥1 AI-referred brief by day 45; AI ≥15% of brief sources by day 90 | Zero AI referrals by day 90 despite indexing → thesis is early; keep the site only as an SEO and data asset | G1 |
| H5 | Buyer-side advocate / price opacity | Families want **price transparency and an advocate** enough to give a detailed brief | Estimator use → brief conversion | ≥25% of estimator users start a brief; ≥50% of starters finish | Under 10% start → interest only; nobody acts | G2 |
| H6 | Credit Karma loop (persistent profile) | Families will **opt in to keep a profile** for future needs | Opt-in checkbox at brief completion | ≥30% opt in | <10% → the need is episodic; drop the profile vision | G2 |
| H7 | Ally, not disruptor | Agencies see us as a **channel, not a threat** | Discovery Q-A6; whether they agree to receive briefs | ≥3 agencies agree to receive v1 briefs | Agencies refuse because they fear disintermediation → the model needs a different supply side (independent nannies plus payroll) | G0 |
| H8 | Willingness to pay (family side) | Some families would **pay for the advocate role** | Fake-door "Concierge+ ($X)" on the confirmation page | ≥5% click | ~0 → monetize the supply side only | G2 |

## Revealed vs. stated preference
Count behavior, not opinions. The sources of truth are: a brief submitted, an opt-in checked, an agency reply received, a fee disclosed in writing, a placement made. Treat "sounds great" in an interview as zero.

## Readout cadence
- Weekly: pipeline counts (`06-measurement.md`).
- At each gate: a one-paragraph verdict per hypothesis — **pass / fail / inconclusive**, and what we do next.
